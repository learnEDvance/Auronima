Auronima is a relational learning interface built around textbooks.

It is NOT:

* a social platform
* an LMS
* a gamified education app
* an AI tutoring system
* or a startup-style “education ecosystem”

Core philosophy:

“People understand concepts better when new ideas are connected meaningfully to things they already understand.”

The system treats textbooks as terrain, and understanding as a network of conceptual relations layered onto that terrain.

Learning should feel exploratory and relational:

* analogies
* prerequisite ideas
* future abstractions
* intuition bridges
* conceptual rabbit holes

Understanding should emerge through connected exploration.
