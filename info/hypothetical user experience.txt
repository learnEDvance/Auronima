# Auronima — Interaction Experience

## Core Principle

Auronima is built around a single idea:

Learning should feel like uncovering an existing structure, where relations become tangible through interaction rather than software.

The learner should rarely feel as though they are opening files, switching screens, or following hyperlinks.

Instead, every interaction should create the feeling that understanding is gradually unfolding from something already present.

The interface remains continuous throughout the entire experience.

---

# Beginning

The learner begins inside a simple resource library.

The library behaves like any familiar media browser.

It contains books, PDFs, videos, and other learning resources.

Nothing about the library is unusual.

The complexity begins only after a resource is opened.

---

# Reading and Watching

Opening a resource places it at the center of the workspace.

Books and PDFs open in reading mode.

Videos open in a standard media player.

Initially, the learner experiences a familiar interface.

There are no floating graphs or visible knowledge networks.

The resource itself remains the primary focus.

---

# Progression

Every resource possesses its own internal progression.

Books and PDFs progress through pages.

Pages exist along the local Z direction.

Moving forward or backward changes which page occupies the current position.

Videos progress through timestamps rather than pages.

Each timestamp behaves as a meaningful location inside the video's own progression.

Regardless of resource type, progression always represents moving through the resource itself rather than leaving it.

---

# Questions Become Objects

Whenever the learner encounters something they wish to understand better, they simply select it.

For books and PDFs, this is usually a highlighted passage or image.

For videos, objects naturally appear when the playback reaches meaningful timestamps.

The learner never searches through menus for additional explanations.

Instead, explanations emerge directly from the material that caused the question.

---

# Objects

Selecting something creates a learning object beside the resource.

A visible dotted thread connects the object to the location from which it emerged.

The thread communicates origin.

It answers a simple question:

*"Where did this come from?"*

Objects may represent many different kinds of learning material.

Examples include:

* interactive diagrams
* simulations
* equations
* experiments
* historical stories
* questions
* analogies
* videos
* books
* PDFs
* illustrations

Every object follows the same interaction model.

Objects may be expanded.

Expanded objects may reveal additional objects.

The learner only learns one rule:

**Expand something to discover what exists inside it.**

---

# Progressive Discovery

Knowledge is never presented all at once.

Objects reveal only what is currently relevant.

As understanding grows, additional structure naturally appears.

Every newly revealed object feels earned through exploration.

The learner should repeatedly experience the feeling:

*"I found this."*

rather than

*"The application opened another window."*

---

# Scale

Objects farther from the original resource gradually become smaller.

This does not indicate importance.

Instead, scale communicates conceptual distance.

The learner naturally zooms toward increasingly specific ideas.

Large foundational ideas remain readable from afar.

Small specialized ideas reveal themselves only through closer inspection.

The world behaves like a continuously explorable landscape.

---

# Continuous Context

The learner never leaves the current exploration.

The original resource always remains visible within the environment.

Objects appear around it rather than replacing it.

Context is never discarded.

Understanding grows outward instead of branching into disconnected screens.

---

# Nested Resources

Books, PDFs, and videos may themselves appear as learning objects.

Initially they behave as embedded references.

They may automatically navigate to the relevant page or timestamp while remaining compact.

Only when the learner intentionally enters them do they unfold into their own explorable environments.

Every resource therefore behaves consistently regardless of whether it was opened from the library or discovered through exploration.

---

# Books and PDFs

When expanded fully, books and PDFs unfold into spatial page terrain.

Pages occupy positions along their own local progression.

Every learning object remains attached to the page from which it originated.

Moving through the pages moves the entire branch of attached objects together.

The learner never loses contextual relationships while progressing through the document.

---

# Videos

Videos unfold through timestamps rather than pages.

Objects appear only when the playback reaches meaningful moments.

The video may automatically pause to allow exploration.

If the learner continues playback while interacting with objects, the video transitions into a picture-in-picture view in the lower-left corner.

Playback continues without interrupting exploration.

Seeking through the timeline moves timestamp positions together with every object attached to them.

The learner experiences a continuous lesson rather than disconnected annotations.

---

# Visible Threads

Visible dotted threads communicate structural origin.

They answer the question:

*"Which object produced this one?"*

Every object has a visible parent.

These structural relationships keep the learner oriented throughout exploration.

---

# Invisible Relationships

The most important relationships inside Auronima are never drawn.

They are behavioral.

When one object causes another object to react, an invisible semantic relationship exists between them.

For example:

An interactive mirror simulation changes.

↓

A property table immediately highlights the matching row.

↓

An equation updates its variables.

↓

A graph reshapes itself.

The learner experiences these reactions as one connected idea.

No visible thread is required.

The relationship is expressed through synchronized behavior.

These invisible behavioral relationships form the true conceptual network of Auronima.

Objects are connected not only because they originate from one another, but because they actively influence one another.

Understanding emerges from observing these interactions.

---

# Exploration

Every object may continue revealing additional objects.

There is no practical depth limit.

Knowledge unfolds recursively.

Each expanded object temporarily becomes the center of its own local exploration while remaining connected to everything that came before it.

Exploration therefore feels continuous regardless of depth.

---

# Collapsing Exploration

Exploration may be closed at any time.

Instead of closing individual windows, the learner selects the visible thread connecting two objects.

Everything beyond that relationship recursively folds inward.

The folding animation always begins at the outermost descendants.

Each level collapses into its parent.

The process continues recursively until only the directly connected object remains.

The learner experiences the branch as naturally folding back into itself.

Closed branches are considered finished explorations.

Their internal state is discarded.

Reopening begins a fresh exploration rather than restoring previous branches.

---

# Navigation

Movement remains physically consistent throughout the environment.

The learner moves freely through space.

Zoom reveals additional detail.

Progression moves through pages or timestamps.

Every interaction preserves orientation instead of replacing one interface with another.

---

# Exploration Map

The Exploration Map is separate from the spatial environment.

It is not a miniature version of the world.

Instead, it records only what the learner has already discovered.

Books, objects, and relationships appear only after they have been explored.

The map represents understanding rather than geography.

It grows alongside the learner.

---

# Returning Home

The learner may return home at any time.

Selecting the home node activates the exploration rope.

The rope traces the exact exploration path that produced the learner's current understanding.

It may be followed step by step through previously explored resources.

The border of the Exploration Map also continuously indicates the direction of home.

Selecting it immediately returns to the home resource.

The rope supports careful retracing.

The border supports rapid return.

---

# Design Goal

The learner should never feel as though they are navigating software.

Every interaction should reinforce the feeling that knowledge already possesses an internal structure waiting to be uncovered.

Objects reveal relationships.

Relationships trigger behaviors.

Behaviors reveal deeper understanding.

The learner does not move between isolated applications.

They continuously explore a living network of connected ideas, where every discovery naturally leads to another without losing context or orientation.
